\# UniRide – Project Scope



\## 1. In Scope

1\. Seat booking and cancellation.

2\. Fee payment status per student.

3\. Trip start and end reporting by drivers.

4\. Passenger list per bus for gate security.



\## 2. Out of Scope

1\. Route planning and optimisation.

2\. Vehicle maintenance management.

3\. Driver payroll.

4\. Building a payment gateway.
