\# UniRide – Three Measurable Objectives



\## 1. Faster Seat Registration

\- Measure: Staff hours spent registering seats at semester start.

\- Target: Reduce registration time from about three working days to under one hour within one term.



\## 2. Fewer Fee Disputes

\- Measure: Number of fee disputes handled by the accounts office per month.

\- Target: Reduce fee disputes by at least 70% within two terms.



\## 3. Less Overcrowding

\- Measure: Percentage of trips carrying more passengers than available seats.

\- Target: Reduce overcrowded trips to under 5% within one term.
