\# Problem Statement – UniRide



Students and staff use approximately 25 university buses to travel to campus. Currently, seats are registered using paper records, and transport fees are paid at the accounts counter. Passengers cannot know the current location of buses or whether seats are available. This causes long waiting times, overcrowding on some routes, and underutilization of others. Fee disputes also consume the time of transport and accounts staff. Parents have no reliable way to confirm whether their children have boarded the bus. The university also lacks accurate information about passenger demand on different routes, making transport management difficult.
